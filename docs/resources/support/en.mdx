# {frontmatter.title}

Where to write depends on which product your question is about. The [Maintenance](/resources/maintenance) page describes how we test, update and fix the code.

---

## Open-Source Plugins, Module and Starter

- **Bugs** – in GitHub issues, in the repository of the [plugins and the module](https://github.com/gorgojs/medusa-integrations/issues) or of the [starter](https://github.com/gorgojs/medusa-dtc-starter/issues).
- **Installation and setup questions** – in the Telegram support chat [@gorgojs_chat](https://t.me/gorgojs_chat).
- **Vulnerabilities** – privately only, as described in [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/gorgojs/medusa-integrations/blob/main/SECURITY.md).

We usually reply within one business day, Monday to Friday.

## What Support Doesn't Cover

- Developing, configuring and integrating a plugin into your project. That work is arranged under a separate contract.
- Questions about Medusa itself that aren't related to our code. Ask them in the Medusa developer chat [@medusajs_chat](https://t.me/medusajs_chat).
